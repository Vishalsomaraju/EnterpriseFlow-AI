System

Deployable enterprise software, not editorial AI and not neon dev tooling.

The shell is neutral and compact. Status colors carry meaning, mono is reserved for traceable artifacts, and the signature motion is isolated to graph and impact surfaces where change propagation matters.

Usage rules

  • Blue is the single product accent family across both themes.
  • Green means automated or passed.
  • Amber means waiting on a human or unresolved review.
  • Purple is reserved for IBM Bob activity and generated artifacts.

Spacing and elevation

Compact shell rules

Spacing scale

4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56

Interface internals stay within 8-20. Page rhythm uses 24-56.

Radius scale

6px controls, 8px badges + rows, 10px cards and panels

No soft consumer rounding. Keep corners disciplined.

Elevation

1px borders first, shadows only for hover or overlay states.

Base shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(16,24,40,0.04)
